Academic Programmes & Curriculum

Comprehensive CBSE curriculum integrated with progressive experiential learning, co-scholastic arts, and structured daily school timetables.

Pre-KG to Grade VIII Montessori & Kindergarten Integrated STEM & Arts Holistic Co-Scholastic Pillars
Academic Rigour

Scholastic Programmes (Pre-KG to Grade VIII)

Our scholastic curriculum is rigorously designed to be comprehensive, stimulating, and aligned with the CBSE framework, ensuring depth of knowledge and academic rigour. We move beyond rote learning to an enquiry-based, skills-focused approach.

Foundation Stage

Montessori / Pre-KG

The Pre-KG curriculum is designed as a smooth bridge between home and formal schooling, anchored in the Montessori Philosophy and enriched with modern early childhood education practices.

Pedagogy
"Learn through Discovery" and "Prepared Environment". Meticulously structured with self-correcting Montessori materials allowing children to develop concentration, coordination, and independence at their own pace.
Focus Areas
Fine and gross motor skills, sensory perception, basic oral communication, number readiness, and Practical Life activities (dressing, pouring, organizing).
Learning Outcome
Students become self-directed, confident learners who are intrinsically motivated and prepared for the transition to formal schooling.
Kindergarten Transition

LKG / UKG

The Kindergarten programme shifts toward a more structured, yet highly engaging, Play-Based and Theme-Based Learning methodology, preparing children for the academic rigour of Grade I.

Pedagogy
Integrated Theme-Based Learning where literacy, numeracy, and environmental awareness are taught through immersive themes, songs, stories, and cooperative play using manipulatives (Cuisenaire rods, unit blocks).
Focus Areas
Foundational literacy (Phonics, sight words, emergent reading), numeracy (number recognition, counting, arithmetic), critical listening, and social-emotional development (sharing, empathy).
Learning Outcome
Strong pre-reading and pre-writing proficiency, conceptual math foundation, and social competence for primary school success.
Primary & Middle School

Grades I - VIII (Pre-CBSE)

A sophisticated blend of Constructivism, Experiential Learning, and Application-Based teaching aligned with CBSE. Grade VI–VIII serves as a Pre-CBSE foundation developing advanced competencies early.

Pedagogy
Inquiry & Project-Based Learning (PBL) with real-world problems. Differentiated instruction addressing visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learners with extensive lab work, debates, and simulations.
Focus Areas
Mastery of core concepts, higher-order thinking skills (HOTS), cyber literacy, scientific inquiry, oral and written articulation, and independent study habits.
Learning Outcome
Competent, critical thinkers equipped with a disciplined scientific mindset, fully prepared for Secondary and Senior Secondary CBSE board exams.
Holistic Balance

Co-Scholastic Programmes

The holistic development of an NPS student is ensured through a wide spectrum of co-scholastic activities that nurture diverse talents and essential life skills, fully integrated into the school day.

Creative Arts

Providing rich avenues for fine motor mastery, visual perception, and artistic confidence:

  • • Fine Arts & Sketching
  • • Clay Sculpture & Pottery
  • • Digital Art & Media Design
  • • Literary & Poetic Expression

Performing Arts

Nurturing rhythm, physical expression, auditory excellence, and stage charisma:

  • • Classical & Contemporary Dance
  • • Instrumental Music (Keyboards, Strings, Percussion)
  • • Classical & Western Vocal Training
  • • Theatre Arts & Dramatic Expression

Intellectual Growth

Stimulating analytical acumen, logical thinking, and global awareness:

  • • Competitive Debating & Elocution
  • • Interschool Quiz Clubs & General Knowledge
  • • STEM Robotics & Automated Circuits
  • • Eco-Clubs & Environmental Stewardship
Campus Routine

School Day Schedule

Thoughtfully structured to optimize learning, engagement, and well-being. A dynamic balance of academic classes, co-scholastic activities, and physical education.

Morning Arrival & Core Academic Session
Dedicated to core academic learning, mathematics, sciences, languages, and conceptual exploration.
07:55 AM – 11:30 AM
Mid-Day Lunch & Social Recreation Break
Nutritious vegetarian meals in the cafeteria and structured outdoor playtime encouraging social bonding.
11:30 AM – 12:15 PM
Afternoon Practical Session & Sports / Co-Scholastic
Hands-on lab experiments, STEM robotics, performing arts, library research, and outdoor sports coaching.
12:15 PM – 03:00 PM
Morning Welcome & Montessori Discovery
Self-directed sensory play, phonics exploration, numeracy songs, and motor skill activities in prepared classrooms.
08:30 AM – 10:30 AM
Snack & Refreshment Time
Healthy vegetarian refreshments fostering eating etiquette, sharing, and peer interaction under teacher care.
10:30 AM – 11:00 AM
Storytelling, Creative Movement & Dismissal
Theme-based play, expressive drama, circle time, and orderly dispersal with mandatory escort verification.
11:00 AM – 12:30 PM
Note: Specific period-wise bell timings are communicated to parents post-admission. School transport buses depart promptly after dispersal.
